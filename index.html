Artist Overview

Arun Adhikari is an Indian electronic music producer, DJ, and vocal artist known for pushing the boundaries of modern sound. Operating at the intersection of cultural rhythms and futuristic beats, he is renowned for blending dynamic genres such as EDM, Bass Music, Moombahton, Desi Hip Hop, and Indian Pop.

With roots in Kathmandu, Nepal, and currently based in Jamshedpur, India, Arun brings a unique cross-cultural perspective to his audio engineering. His music aesthetic heavily influences both his sonic architecture and visual identity, making his live performances and studio releases a true electronic experience.

CAREER

Arun Adhikari began his journey in music with a deep passion for electronic sound design and modern music production. Over the years, he has developed a distinctive style that blends EDM, Bass Music, Moombahton, Desi Hip Hop, and Indian Pop into a powerful and energetic sonic experience.

Starting as an independent producer, Arun experimented with various digital audio workstations and production techniques, gradually shaping his signature sound. His work focuses on combining global electronic influences with South Asian musical elements, creating a sound that resonates with both local and international audiences.

Beyond studio production, Arun Adhikari is also recognized as a DJ and vocal artist. His performances emphasize immersive sound, rhythmic intensity, and innovative audio engineering. Through his music releases and live sets, he continues to explore new creative directions in the evolving world of electronic music.

PERSONAL LIFE

Outside of his professional music career, Arun Adhikari maintains a strong interest in technology, digital creativity, and online media. His passion for programming and modern internet culture has also influenced his artistic vision and branding as a music creator.

Born in Jamshedpur, India, with family roots in Kathmandu, Nepal, Arun carries a multicultural background that shapes his artistic perspective. This cross-cultural influence plays an important role in the way he approaches sound design and musical storytelling.

In addition to music production, Arun focuses on expanding his digital presence, building connections with listeners worldwide, and developing new creative projects that bridge music, technology, and global culture.
